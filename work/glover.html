Design process

From research to realisation

Every project starts with research, which defines the problem and sets the design criteria. From there, the work moved through a series of steps from initial concept to finished design.

User personas

Built from conversations with users, the personas kept the team focused on who we were designing for.

Paper sketches

To get ideas out of my head early, I start by drawing the basic flow of the app on paper.

Wireframes

Wireframes set out the skeleton of the app, arranging elements to best serve each screen's purpose before any visual design.

Visual design

Imagery, colour, shape, typography and form, used to improve usability and the overall experience.
