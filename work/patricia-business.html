Patricia Business · Fintech

A crypto payment gateway for merchants

Patricia Business is a B2B crypto payment gateway that lets merchants accept Bitcoin, with instant payments, transparent pricing, customer management and easy payouts.

Company
Patricia
Role
Senior Product Designer, design lead
When
2020 – 2022
Where
Lagos, Nigeria

Overview

Helping merchants accept Bitcoin

Patricia Business is a crypto payment processor and was one of the fastest-growing businesses in Nigeria. As Bitcoin use rose across Africa, more merchants began accepting it as payment, and Patricia Business was their gateway: instant payments, simple and transparent pricing, customer management and easy payouts.

Approach

Understanding the core problem first

Before any ideation, I gather as much information about the project as I can, working hand in hand with the product manager and user researcher on flows and a shared idea of what to look for.

I led a team of three designers (one UX researcher and two product designers) and set the design direction, working closely with the researcher and product manager on user flows and wireframes. I also built the design system while designing key pages: the dashboard, wallet pages and payment links.

Customer journey

After the research, we brought all the findings together into a customer journey.

Ideate

We sketched a lot to test directions and give the product a structure, working from a list of screens that covered every scenario. I shared sketches with the team for feedback.

User flow

By this point we understood the users and the core functions, but had no definite structure. This stage organised everything from research into a flow, thinking through each step on the user's path. It started on paper and then moved to digital.

Key screens

Dashboard and payment links

Dashboard

Merchants see their BTC wallet and local wallet side by side, a graph of transactions, their transaction history and their next payout. The side navigation gives access to every other feature.

Payment links

Once merchants integrate Patricia Business into their online store, they can send payment links to customers or add them to products on their website.
