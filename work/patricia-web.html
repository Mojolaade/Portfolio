Seamless crypto payments and trading for Africa

Overview

Spend Bitcoin like any other currency

Patricia is the largest cryptocurrency payment company in Africa. It offers digital services such as crypto trading, storage and payments. When users sign up, they get a wallet to trade or store crypto like Bitcoin and Ethereum.

What makes Patricia different is that users can spend their Bitcoin like fiat money. Each customer gets a debit card connected to their crypto wallet, and every payment made with the card is charged directly to that wallet.

Patricia is built on the idea that the faster and easier people can reach essential digital services, the better their quality of life. I worked with the Patricia team to design the overall UI, fix the information architecture and improve the experience of the old app.

The problem

An app that had outgrown its design

A growing customer base, new services and new business needs had left the web app cluttered, hard to navigate and confusing. Many features had been added that the original user flows and design never planned for. Readability and responsiveness on mobile, and screen transitions, needed work too.

Patricia needed a new way to present its services: easy to navigate, accessible to all its users, and scalable enough to take on more features in the future.

Who it's for

Visual design

The Patricia crypto app

The app is designed so users can find every feature intuitively. It takes only a few clicks to get from the homepage to transactions, wallets and other key screens, and the layout stays simple and friendly.

Sign up and log in

Account creation takes a few short steps: email, a secure password with live checks, and country. Users are then invited to add two-factor authentication.

Dashboard

The experience is designed so anyone, of any age, can use every feature. Based on feedback, I made the wallet balance, recent activity, wallets, cards and the user's KYC level reachable straight from the dashboard.

Wallets

The main wallets are the crypto wallets and the fiat wallet. Users needed to reach transactions easily, so sending and receiving BTC happens right from the BTC wallet, and the fiat wallet's own actions, deposit and transfer, sit on the fiat wallet.

Cards and referrals

The physical card screen shows the user's Patricia card alongside key details and the actions they need to control it, such as freezing, blocking or replacing it. The virtual card screen shows the user's virtual cards, card details, card actions and recent transactions.

Referrals and rewards show the points a user has earned, their place on the leaderboard and the friends they've invited.

Buy and sell

The Buy BTC and Sell BTC screens let users enter a dollar or crypto amount, then confirm the transaction. Other products, such as airtime top-ups, live alongside.

Security and verification

Security features protect user accounts, including a live selfie for proof of life and step-by-step identity verification that raises the user's daily limits.

On mobile

Every screen was designed to be responsive, so sign-up, login, password recovery and the dashboard read just as well on a phone.

Impact

Loved for how it looks and how it works

The client valued the new web app, especially the care taken to make workflows interesting and intuitive for crypto traders. Uncertainty and fear around crypto dropped significantly, as new users now get instructional material on what crypto is and how to use the app.

The app stayed functional without giving up a modern, attractive look. That balance made it popular with both existing and new users.
