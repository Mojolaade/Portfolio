About

Bringing order to complexity

I work across the full arc of product design, from early-stage discovery and research to production-ready execution, for start-ups building from zero and for complex platforms carrying years of technical debt.

My background is in Philosophy, where I trained to interrogate assumptions and build arguments that hold up under pressure. That still shapes how I work: I ask what is actually true about how people and products behave, not just what looks right on a canvas.

System operations
